02 / THE LAUNCH
KRISHI RATNA LEAGUE — BENGAL

Krishi Ratna League Bengal

Announced from the stage at 09:50 by MahAcharya Shri. sourabh J. sarkar, and placed before Bengal by Smt. Rinku Majumder Ghosh in her acceptance address.

Across West Bengal, the League will mobilise and train 5,000 farmers in global cutting-edge Smart Farming and Integrated Farming Systems (IFS). The training is structured. What follows it is a competition — objective, transparent — and the finest performers are conferred prestigious awards.

The intent is plain: that a farmer's skill should be measured in the open, and recognised in the open.

03 / THE ACCEPTANCETHE BACKBONE OF BENGAL

Empowering women in smart farming

Smt. Rinku Majumder Ghosh accepted the office of Adhyakshaa, Mahila Wing, Bharatiya Krishak Samaj West Bengal, and delivered the acceptance address that named the League.

“I firmly declare that in West Bengal, our mothers and sisters are the true backbone of the agricultural ecosystem. From sunrise to sunset — from dawn until dusk — they toil relentlessly in the fields, battling alongside the soil and nurturing our crops with unmatched devotion. Yet, with the very same grace, they manage their homes, care for their elders and children, and shoulder total family responsibility.”

06 / ROOTS TO POSSIBILITIESAnnounced by Smt. Reena J. Sarkar at 10:45.
PROTYABARTAN · প্রত্যাবর্তন · THE HOMECOMING

Protyabartan

প্রত্যাবর্তন

Krishak Samaj Durga Puja 2026

At Sector V (Munshi Bheri, Salt Lake), Krishak Samaj Durga Puja 2026 will carry a comprehensive live model of the Krishi Ratna League — a working integrated farm, built on the pandal ground.

It will demonstrate how modern agrarian techniques unlock extraordinary productivity from compact landholdings: a sapling guided from seed to harvest to direct market linkage, generating farm surplus and lifting rural households into genuine prosperity.

Protyabartan is a three-year trilogy. Its core philosophy is Roots to Possibilities, and Possibilities to Prosperity.

Visitors are invited, with their families, to Sector V during Sharodotsav to see the integrated farm model first-hand.
